## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🎙️ Voice213 — AI Voice & Agent Platform
**Founder & CEO | June 2026 – Present**

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-voice213.com-FF7900?style=flat-square)](https://voice213.com)

An AI-powered platform for voice generation, multilingual content creation, and intelligent conversational agents.

**Highlights:**
- AI voice generation for Algerian Darija and regional Arabic dialects
- Voice-based and chat-based AI agents for business automation
- Speech synthesis, transcription, and content generation
- Multilingual AI interactions and workflow orchestration
- Scalable SaaS architecture for creators and businesses
- Platform serving **40,000+ users and 1,000+ clients**

**Technologies:** Next.js, TypeScript, Python, MongoDB, Speech AI, LLMs, and Cloud Infrastructure

### 🧩 MenaReward — Creator Marketing Platform

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-menareward.com-blue?style=flat-square)](https://menareward.com)

A technology platform connecting brands with content creators through performance-driven marketing campaigns.

**Focus areas:**
- Creator-brand collaboration workflows
- Campaign management and performance analytics
- Social platform integrations
- Automated creator operations

### 🗣️ KasbahTTS — Algerian Darija Speech AI

An open-source initiative focused on developing natural and expressive Text-to-Speech models for **Algerian Arabic (Darija)**.

**Research focus:**
- Low-resource language speech synthesis
- Dialect-aware voice generation
- Speech dataset preparation and model fine-tuning
- Improving accessibility of AI speech technologies for North African communities

### 🪞 Lumi — AI Smart Mirror System
**Bachelor's Graduation Project | 2025**

An intelligent smart mirror combining Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Human-Computer Interaction.

**Key capabilities:**
- Real-time hand gesture and body pose recognition
- AI-powered facial and skin analysis
- Deep learning with ResNet-50 architectures
- Contactless physiological signal estimation using rPPG
- Face authentication and interactive voice assistance

**Technologies:** Python, Next.js, MediaPipe, face-api.js, ResNet-50, pyVHR
